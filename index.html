Dear Mummy,

HAPPY BIRTHDAY 💗

Nuv thitey thitlu chala miss avutuna e madya, nuv nanu kotadam manesav parledu le intiki ochaka thanulu thinta 😋

Nuvvu maa intlo Amma maatrame kaadhu… Chef 👩🏻‍🍳 + Doctor 🩺 + Security 🛡️ + Google 🔍 + MENTAL Therapist + Alarm Clock ⏰ + Full-time godava Department 😂 — anni nuvve!

Nenu edaina problem chepthe, first solution ivvadam kanna mundu “Nenu chepthey vinavu niku nachinatu chesthav?” ani oka lecture free ga isthav 😂 Sometimes nenu nee value ni cheppalenu… Sometimes nenu thank you kuda cheppanu… Sometimes ninnu chiraku padatha chiraku tepistha, godava chestha, anavasaram ga kopam techukunta… 😂

naa life lo nenu entha lucky anedi naaku telusu — Endhuku antey nuv natho vunav. ❤️

Naa happiness kosam nuvvu chese chinna chinna sacrifices anni naaku eppudu gurthuntayi. Na kopam ni nuvu matrame bharinchagalavu... Nenu gelisthe naakanna ekkuva happy ayye person kuda nuvve. ❤️

Nuvu maku WONDER WOMEN vi endhuku antey ma life lo nuv oka pedda wonder vi🌝 So today, please no kangaru, no pani, no tension… Just smile, be safe, enjoy your day and konchem nannu kuda disturb cheyyakunda birthday enjoy cheyyi 😂

Happy Birthday Ammaaa! 💌 Love you more . ❤️
